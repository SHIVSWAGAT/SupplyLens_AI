Supply chain AI assistant reference

Prioritize shipments with delay risk above 0.65 for manual review and customer communication.

If weather risk is elevated, prefer safer alternate routes over the cheapest route when service penalties are likely.

Port or hub congestion usually requires proactive ETA updates, dock rebooking, and closer carrier coordination.

Escalate critical shipments when delay minutes are growing and both congestion and weather signals are elevated.

For route optimization, compare risk score, estimated hours, and cost together instead of optimizing a single metric in isolation.
